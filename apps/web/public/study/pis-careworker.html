Kia ora,

I am a final-year engineering student at the University of Auckland, and I would like to invite you to take part in a research study. Please read this sheet before deciding. Taking part is entirely voluntary.

What is this study about?

We have built an app that answers questions about dementia care from a library of trusted New Zealand information. It can answer as text on screen, or as an animated character that speaks the answer aloud.

We want to know whether the speaking character makes the information easier to use, or simply makes it slower. You are being invited as part of a smaller group of people who work in dementia or aged care, alongside a larger group of family carers. Comparing the two groups tells us whether the app adapts usefully to the different things a professional and a family member need from it.

Why me, and is my employer involved?

You have been invited through [recruitment channel] because you work in dementia or aged care.

**This study is not about you or your practice, and it is not an assessment of your knowledge.** We are testing the software. Your employer is not involved, will not be told whether you took part, and will not receive any individual results.

What would I have to do?

Everything is online, on your own device, at a time that suits you. There is no appointment and no observer.

  1. You receive a link and an access code.
  2. The site explains the study and asks for your consent.
  3. The app issues you a participant code and asks you to keep it. No name is collected, so it is the only way your data can be located — you would need it to request deletion.
  4. A few short background questions — no name, no contact details.
  5. Six short scenarios. For each, you use the app to find out what could help. Three are done using the speaking avatar, three using text chat.
  6. A usability questionnaire after each of the two halves.
  7. Five short open questions at the end.

    8. About 35 to 45 minutes, with a break available between halves.

    You will need a device with a microphone and internet access, and Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge — the voice features do not work reliably in other browsers.

Any risks?

The risks are low. The scenarios are fictional and you are not asked about specific patients, residents or clients. **Please do not enter any real names or any information about a real person in your care** — doing so would breach their privacy, not just yours.

The subject matter may still be difficult. Support is available:
111 (emergency) · Healthline 0800 611 116 · Alzheimers NZ 0800 004 001 ·
1737 (free call or text).

The app provides general information only. It is not clinical decision support, it is not validated for clinical use, and it must not be used to inform care for a real person. It is a research prototype and can be wrong.

Benefits

No direct benefit and no payment. Your professional perspective is particularly valuable in judging whether the answers the app gives are accurate, appropriately cautious, and usable in practice.

What is collected?

Questionnaire answers; time taken and number of questions asked per task; **the questions you asked and the answers the app gave**; and technical data (browser, response times).

The app saves conversations so a user can return to them, stored against an anonymous account identifier rather than a name. That is separate from whether the
researcher may read yours, which the consent form asks on its own.

To generate answers, your text is sent to OpenAI, and the spoken voice is generated by ElevenLabs — both US providers, neither given your name or contact details, and the project account is not on a tier that trains on submitted text.

We do not collect your name, email, IP address, employer, or any audio. Speech is converted to text and only text is retained. Everything is stored against your participant code.

Researcher access to your conversation is consented to separately on the consent form. You can decline it and still take part.

Data is held securely, accessible only to the researcher and supervisor, retained six years per University policy and then destroyed. Anonymised findings appear in my project report and may be presented or published. Nothing identifying you will be published.

Withdrawal

You may stop at any time, without giving a reason and without disadvantage, using the
"I need to stop" button on every screen.

You may request deletion of your data up to two weeks after your session by emailing the researcher with your participant code. After that the data cannot be identified as yours.
