Information Sheet

For the support person accompanying a participant living with dementia

Department of Electrical, Computer and Software Engineering, University of Auckland

Project title: Evaluating an avatar-based assistant for finding dementia-care information
Researcher: Richman Tan · Supervisor: Assoc. Prof. Jing Sun

Letterhead, Head of Department contact, UAHPEC reference and approval date to be added before submission.

Kia ora,

Someone you support has been invited to take part in this study. This sheet explains what your role would be. Please read it alongside their information sheet (participant-information-sheet-plwd.md), which is written for them.

Why a support person is required

For participants living with dementia, this study cannot go ahead without a support person present for the whole session. There are three reasons, and they are the reasons the ethics committee will care about:

  1. Consent needs to be ongoing, not a one-off signature. Someone who knows the person is far better placed than a form to notice if they no longer want to continue.
  2. The session is otherwise unsupervised. Nobody from the research team is present. If the person becomes confused, tired or upset, you are the only person there.
  3. The topic is dementia. It may raise difficult feelings, and it is not reasonable to have someone encounter that alone in front of a screen.

What we would ask of you

  • Be present for the whole session — roughly 20 minutes.
  • Confirm at the start that the person understands what they are agreeing to and is willing. You will be asked to sign the consent form alongside them.
  • Check again before the second half. The website will prompt you. If they have had enough, stop — that is a completely acceptable outcome and is not a failed session.
  • Help if they ask you to, and make a mental note of what you had to help with. We will ask you about it at the end, and it is genuinely useful data — knowing where someone needed help is exactly what tells us the app is not accessible enough.
  • Stop the session if they seem distressed, tired, or are no longer engaged, even if they have not said so. Use the "I need to stop" button. You do not need anyone's permission and you do not need to explain.

What we would ask you not to do

  • Please do not answer for them or steer them toward the "right" answer. If they cannot find something, that is a finding about the app, and it is the finding we most need.
  • Please do not enter real names or personal details into the app on their behalf.

Your own contribution

At the end we will ask you two short questions:

  1. Was there anything you had to help with?
  2. Was there anything you noticed that they found difficult?

    3. Your answers are recorded against the participant code, and are reported in the same anonymous way as everything else.

A note on what the app is

It is a research prototype that provides general information only. It is not medical advice, it cannot assess anyone's situation, and it can be wrong. Nothing it says should be acted on without speaking to a health professional.
