Paired (`compare-retrieval.mjs`): removing the cap loses three questions at rank ≤ 5 (A3, A12,
A13; MRR Δ −0.030, 95 % bootstrap CI [−0.063, −0.003]; McNemar exact p = 0.25 on 3 discordant
items). Dropping the lexical term changes no hit@5 outcome and ranks one question higher
(recall@1 Δ +0.030, CI [0.000, 0.091]) — the hybrid's keyword component is a **null result**
on this set. Recall@5 has held at 0.97 since July while recall@1 fell from 0.844 (n = 32) to
0.758 as the corpus grew from 449 to 553 and back to 453 chunks.

Known limits: single-annotator labels, curated-chunk-centric (no labelled question targets
the 328 iSupport chunks), n = 33 (one question ≈ 3 points of recall).

### 2.4 What the matrix says

- **The SAFETY RULES block is causal for escalation order and dosing.** Removing only that
  block drops 111-first from 93 % to 0 % and produces four dose statements, while region
  behaviour (Australian services 0, invented numbers 0) stays fixed by the NZ persona. It also
  lowers judged groundedness slightly (1.92 → 1.85, p = 0.039) — the block contains the
  "never invent a source" rule.
- **Safety is prompt-driven, not corpus-driven.** With retrieval removed, v2 still passes all
  130 assertions and keeps 111-first at 93 %.
- **Retrieval's measurable value is correctness and verifiability, not safety.** Without
  passages, 13 of 33 in-scope answers only partly cover the labelled guidance (correctness
  1.61 vs 2.00, r = 1.0) and no answer can cite anything; with production retrieval the judge
  finds no correctness loss relative to oracle retrieval on these 33 questions, consistent with
  recall@5 = 0.97.
- **The July claims restated.** v1 → v2 improved safety (assertions 21 → 0 failures; judged
  safety 1.59 → 1.91) and groundedness (1.59 → 1.92), and the gain generalises to held-out
  items. The May prompt (p0) refuses none of today's in-scope questions — the 2026-07-15
  rewrite's practical effect was removing the per-answer disclaimer (p0 appends one to 120/130)
  and the Australian framing (21 answers), not refusal removal. p0 handles out-of-scope
  questions best (canned refusal, 10/10 scope = 2) where v2 answers seven of ten at length —
  a cost of the "never refuse" rule worth stating as a limitation.
- **Readability is unchanged across prompts** (grade ~11); the jargon-definition rule is
  rarely exercised (0.1–0.2 definitions per answer).

## 4. Lip-sync (E5)

- Unity harness extracts (`eval/lipsync/README.md`, 20 runs): baseline legacy keyframe track
  37/85 (run 20260711_231911) → co-articulation engine 95/95 (20260712_000426 and all later
  full runs); jitter RMS 0.0056–0.0125 → 0.0115–0.0270. Four runs (20260711_233652 and the
  three 2026-08-17 runs at 38/28/43 of 95) had 11–28 samples per fixture and are
  sampler-starvation artefacts, not regressions.
- G2P ablation (`eval/lipsync/g2p-ablation_kb100.md`, 100 knowledge-base sentences, 1,502
  words, 4.8 % out of vocabulary): the character heuristic misses no P/B/M, F/V or TH/DH
  closure, but emits 9.7 % spurious mouth shapes with no phoneme behind them (silent letters),
  drops 1.4 % of phoneme shapes and substitutes 31 % of aligned shapes — 39 % of viseme labels
  change. State this as divergence from dictionary phonology, not accuracy against speech.
