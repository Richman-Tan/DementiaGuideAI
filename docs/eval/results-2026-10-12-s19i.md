## Reading

Ordering escalation first removes the cost of checking back. 111 came first as often as with no rule
(55 against 53), and the model checked back four times as often as the deployed prompt.

It checked back less than the restatement-first rule did (28 against 71). The two orderings trade
check-backs against escalation, and only the escalate-first ordering keeps escalation.

"Escalate first, then check back" is therefore supported for escalation and partly supported for
checking back.
