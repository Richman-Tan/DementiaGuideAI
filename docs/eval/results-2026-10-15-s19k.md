## Reading

- Making 111 available, first and prominent, changed nothing: without instructions that set
  escalation, the model put 111 first in no emergency answer, and named it no more often. Where
  escalation appears in an answer is set by the instructions, not by what the passages offer.
- The one foreign number named the UK's 999 beside 111. The lead passage is labelled with its
  source, NHS UK, which plausibly prompted it; a passage's provenance label can carry a jurisdiction
  cue of its own.

## Limitations

- One passage, one model, 30 items; the comparator is §19E's run of five days earlier.
- Spend: about US$2.
