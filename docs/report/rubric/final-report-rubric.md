# Part IV Project — Final Report marking rubric (2026)

Faculty of Engineering and Design, 2026 Part IV Project Final Report Rubric.
Source PDF: [`2026-p4p-final-report-rubric.pdf`](2026-p4p-final-report-rubric.pdf)
(supplied 2026-09-21). This markdown is a transcription so the criteria can be
grepped and diffed; **the PDF is authoritative** if the two ever disagree.

Bands: **A** 100–80 % (high quality) · **B** 79–65 % (good) · **C** 64–50 %
(satisfactory) · **D** 49–0 % (poor).

## Weightings at a glance

| Criterion | Typical report mapping | Weight | LO |
|---|---|---:|---|
| **A** Literature & Field Knowledge | Introduction, Literature review, Methodology, Discussion | 10 % | LO 1 |
| **B** Problem Definition & Research Framing | Research Question, Aims and Objectives | 10 % | LO 2 |
| **C** Study Design | Literature review, Methods | 20 % | LO 3 |
| **D** Study Execution, Findings & Evaluation | Methods, Results, Discussion | 30 % | LO 4 & 5 |
| **E** Interpretation, Contribution / Impact | Abstract, Discussion, Conclusion | 25 % | LO 8 |
| **F** Technical Writing & Research Communication | Entire report | 5 % | LO 6 |

Execution + interpretation (**D + E**) is **55 %** of the mark. Framing and design
(**A + B + C**) is **40 %**. Writing is **5 %**.

---

## A — Literature & Field Knowledge (10 %, LO 1)

*"What is known or has already been done that is relevant"* — Introduction,
Literature review, Methodology, Discussion.

| Band | Descriptor |
|---|---|
| **A** | References relevant literature and demonstrates a strong, intellectually mature understanding of the research field and related work. Through: retrieval and assessment of the relevant literature; clear and insightful connections across the field; strong evaluation of relevance and implications of prior work. |
| **B** | Good understanding of the research field. Through: retrieval and assessment of a good portion of the relevant literature; identification of some relationships across the field; reasonable evaluation of relevance. |
| **C** | Basic understanding of the research field. Through: descriptive use of a limited selection of relevant literature, or use of some inappropriate literature; weak connections across the field; limited evaluation of relevance. |
| **D** | Significant weaknesses in understanding the research field. Through: minimal or inappropriate use of literature, with poor understanding of key concepts; no clear connections; no meaningful evaluation. |

## B — Problem Definition & Research Framing (10 %, LO 2)

*"What did I want to do, and how does it fit with existing knowledge/practice"* —
Research Question, Aims and Objectives.

| Band | Descriptor |
|---|---|
| **A** | Strong ability to frame and define the research problem. Through: strong critique of literature identifying clear and significant gaps (where applicable) or clear logical progression from literature to research direction; clear, coherent problem statement demonstrating strong knowledge and understanding of subject matter; well-formulated and aligned research questions and hypotheses. |
| **B** | Good ability to frame and define a research problem. Through: clear critique of literature identifying gaps (where applicable) or some logical progression from literature to research direction; well-developed problem statement; appropriate research questions and hypotheses. |
| **C** | Basic ability. Through: limited critique of literature identifying gaps (where applicable) or limited linkage from literature to research direction; basic or unclear problem statement; partially developed research questions. |
| **D** | Significant weaknesses. Through: little or no critique with failure to identify gaps or lack of linkage from literature to research direction; missing or unclear problem statement; absent or inappropriate research questions. |

## C — Study Design (20 %, LO 3)

*"What I planned to do, and why this plan makes sense"* — Literature review, Methods.

| Band | Descriptor |
|---|---|
| **A** | Strong justification (through theory and/or best practice) of study design. Through: strong synthesis of theory, methods, and procedures; clear coherence between theory, methodology, and research/study questions; rigorous study design and method development; well-argued choice of data (where applicable); high-level proficiency in tools and hardware/software co-design (where applicable). |
| **B** | Good understanding of theory and study design. Through: good synthesis of theory and methods; clear alignment with research questions; well-developed research/study design and method development; good discussion of choice of data (where applicable); competent use of tools and integration (where applicable). |
| **C** | Basic understanding. Through: limited synthesis of theory and methods; partial alignment to research questions; workable but underdeveloped design; basic discussion of choice of data (where applicable); basic or inconsistent tool use (where applicable). |
| **D** | Significant weaknesses. Through: incorrect or minimal use of theory and methods; poor alignment with research questions; flawed or inappropriate design; lack of discussion of choice of data (where applicable); ineffective tool use (where applicable). |

## D — Study Execution, Findings & Evaluation (30 %, LO 4 & 5)

*"What I did, what were my results, and how I analysed my findings"* — Methods,
Results, Discussion.

| Band | Descriptor |
|---|---|
| **A** | Strong creative ability / originality to develop / apply methods and evaluate the findings. Through: rigorous and efficient execution of experiments / models / simulations with sufficient scope and breadth; advanced application of data analysis (where applicable); well-formulated arguments based on strong and sustained evidence / findings; diagrams, graphs and tables included where appropriate to support evaluation; critical interpretation, comparison with literature, and evaluation of validity. |
| **B** | Good creative ability / originality. Through: effective execution of experiments / models / simulations with adequate scope; appropriate use of data analysis techniques (where applicable); clear arguments mostly backed up with evidence / findings; some diagrams, graphs and tables included to support evaluation where appropriate; logical interpretation with some evaluation of validity. |
| **C** | Basic creative ability / originality. Through: inconsistent execution; basic use of data analysis techniques (where applicable); understandable but sometimes unclear presentation; some lack of supporting material for evaluation, where diagrams, graphs and tables would have been helpful; descriptive interpretation with limited evaluation. |
| **D** | Significant weaknesses. Through: poor or incomplete execution; minimal or incorrect analysis techniques / models / simulations; disorganised use of data analysis techniques (where applicable); significant gaps in arguments, with lack of diagrams, graphs or tables to support evaluation; little or no evaluation. |

## E — Interpretation, Contribution / Impact (25 %, LO 8)

*"Discussion: What my study showed, and why does it matter?"* — Abstract,
Discussion, Conclusion.

| Band | Descriptor |
|---|---|
| **A** | High level of critical thinking, a strong ability to interpret findings and meaningful contribution to knowledge or real-world connection. Through: highly knowledgeable, logical, and expert interpretation; meaningful contribution to existing knowledge or clear connection to real-world problems (as applicable); strong articulation of significance, limitations, and improvements; **claims are proportionate to findings, contributions, and impact**; insightful conclusions and recommendations. |
| **B** | Good level of critical thinking, ability to interpret findings, and some contribution to knowledge or real-world connection. Through: logical and coherent interpretation; clear contribution to knowledge or connection to real-world linkage articulated (as applicable); good articulation of significance; may have minor overstatement of findings, contributions, or impact; reasonable conclusions. |
| **C** | Basic level. Through: descriptive interpretation; limited contribution or minimal real-world connection articulated (as applicable); weak or exaggerated articulation of importance; may overstate findings, contributions, or impact; basic conclusions. |
| **D** | Significant weaknesses; no contribution to knowledge or real-world connection. Through: little or no interpretation; no contribution to knowledge or real-world linkage (as applicable); no or exaggerated articulation of importance; significantly overstates findings, contributions, or impact; missing or unsupported conclusions. |

## F — Technical Writing & Research Communication (5 %, LO 6)

Entire report.

| Band | Descriptor |
|---|---|
| **A** | Strong proficiency in technical communication. Through: clear, coherent, and professionally structured report; precise and appropriate technical language; logical flow and organisation; complete and well-organised research compendium; high standard of presentation with no significant errors. |
| **B** | Good technical writing proficiency. Through: clear and well-structured writing; appropriate technical language; logical organisation; complete supporting material; minor errors only. |
| **C** | Basic proficiency. Through: generally understandable writing; inconsistent technical language; some structural issues; incomplete support material; noticeable errors. |
| **D** | Significant weaknesses. Through: unclear or poorly structured writing; incorrect language use; disorganised presentation; missing supporting materials; frequent errors affecting clarity. |
