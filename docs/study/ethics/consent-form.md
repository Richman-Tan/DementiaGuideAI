## Standard form — carers and health/aged-care workers

Presented on screen as individual tick boxes. Every box must be ticked to proceed,
**except item 9**, which is optional and does not block participation.

Note the split introduced by items 7 and 9. The app **saves conversations as a
normal part of working** — that is not something a participant can opt out of while
using it, and pretending otherwise would be misleading. What is genuinely optional
is whether the **researcher** may read them. Those are two different questions and
the form asks them separately.

I have read the Participant Information Sheet, have understood the nature of the
research and why I have been selected. I have had the opportunity to ask questions
and have had them answered to my satisfaction.

☐ **1.** I agree to take part in this research.

☐ **2.** I understand that taking part is voluntary, and that I may stop at any time
without giving a reason and without any disadvantage.

☐ **3.** I understand that the situations I am given are fictional, and that I should
not enter real names or any information that would identify me or anyone else.

☐ **4.** I understand that the app provides general information only, that it is not
medical advice, that it is a research prototype which may give incomplete or
incorrect answers, and that I should consult a health professional about any real
decision.

☐ **5.** I agree to the collection of my questionnaire responses, the time I take on
each task, the number of questions I ask, and technical information about my browser
and the app's response times.

☐ **6.** I understand that no audio recording of my voice is kept, and that anything
I say to the app is converted to text with only the text retained.

☐ **7.** I understand that **the app saves my conversations** so that I can come
back to them, in the same way any messaging app does, and that they are stored
against an anonymous account identifier rather than my name.

☐ **8.** I understand that answering my questions means my words are sent to
overseas service providers (OpenAI, and ElevenLabs for the spoken voice), that
they receive no name or contact details, and that the project account is not on
any tier that uses submitted text to train models.

☐ **9.** *(Optional — you may decline this and still take part.)*
Separately from the app saving them, I agree that **the researcher may read my
conversations from this session**, to check whether the app answered correctly and
safely.
☐ Yes, the researcher may read them   ☐ No, they may not

☐ **10.** I understand that my study data will be stored against a participant code
and not my name, held securely for six years, and then destroyed.

☐ **11.** I understand that I may request deletion of my data up to two weeks after my
session by contacting the researcher and quoting my participant code, and that after
that point my data cannot be identified.

☐ **12.** I understand that anonymised findings will be reported in the researcher's
project report and may be presented or published, and that nothing identifying me
will be included.
