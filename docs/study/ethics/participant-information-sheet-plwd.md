## For people living with dementia

*Department of Electrical, Computer and Software Engineering, University of Auckland*

## An invitation

**Kia ora.**

My name is Richman. I am a student at the University of Auckland.

I would like to invite you to help with a research project.

You do not have to say yes. It is your choice.

Take as much time as you need to decide. You can talk it over with your family or
your support person.

---

## What is it about?

I have helped build an app that answers questions about dementia.

It can answer in two ways.

- One way, it **writes** the answer on the screen.
- The other way, a **person on the screen talks** to you and says the answer out loud.

I want to know which way is easier to use.

---

## What would I do?

You would use the app on a computer or a tablet.

Someone you trust — a family member or support person — will sit with you the whole
time.

I would ask you to look up **two things** using the app. One with the writing. One
with the talking person.

Then I would ask you a few short questions about what you thought.

**It takes about 20 minutes.** You can stop and have a rest whenever you want.

---

## Do I have to answer anything I do not want to?

No.

You can skip any question.

You can stop at any time. You do not have to say why. Nothing bad will happen.

Just say so, or press the button that says **"I need to stop"**.

---

## Is there anything I should know?

The app is **not** a doctor.

It gives general information only. It cannot tell you what to do about your own
health.

Always talk to your doctor or nurse about anything to do with your own health.

The app is new and still being tested. Sometimes it gets things wrong. Finding that
out is part of why I am doing this.

---

## Will anyone know it was me?

No.

I will not write down your name.

I will not keep a recording of your voice.

The app remembers what you say to it. That is so you can look back at it later, like
a messages app does. It is saved without your name.

I would also like to **read** what you said to the app, and what it said back. That
helps me check the app gave good answers. You can say no to that and still take part.

To answer you, the app sends your words to two companies overseas. They do not get
your name.

Everything is kept safe. Only my supervisor and I can see it.

I will write about what I found out in my project report. **Your name will never be
in it.**

---

## If you feel upset

Talking about dementia can be hard.

If you would like to talk to someone:

- **111** — if it is an emergency
- **Healthline 0800 611 116** — a nurse, any time, free
- **Alzheimers NZ 0800 004 001** — for advice and support
- **1737** — free call or text, any time, to talk to someone

---

## Changing your mind

You can change your mind at any time.

If you want me to delete what you did, tell me **within two weeks** and I will.
