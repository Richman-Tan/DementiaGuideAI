# Recruitment Materials

All approaches go **through a referring service**, never direct from the researcher.
Nothing here may be sent before UAHPEC approval, and the approval reference must
appear on each item.

---

## 1. Email to a referring organisation

*To Alzheimers NZ / Dementia NZ regional services, and the project partner's networks.
Sent by the researcher; asks the organisation to forward, not to hand over contacts.*

**Subject:** University of Auckland research — would you be able to share this with carers?

> Kia ora,
>
> My name is Richman Tan. I am a final-year engineering student at the University of
> Auckland, supervised by Assoc. Prof. Jing Sun, working on a project about making
> dementia-care information easier to find.
>
> We have built an app that answers carers' questions using a library of trusted New
> Zealand information. It can answer either as text, or as an animated character that
> speaks the answer aloud. We would like to find out which works better for the people
> who would actually use it — that is the whole study.
>
> I am writing to ask whether you would be willing to pass the attached invitation on
> to family carers connected with your service. I am not asking for any contact
> details, and I will not approach anyone directly — people would get in touch with me
> if they are interested.
>
> What is involved for a participant:
>
> - About 35–45 minutes, online, on their own device, whenever suits them
> - Six short made-up scenarios; they use the app to find information
> - A few questions afterwards about how easy it was
> - No names collected, no payment, and they can stop at any time
>
> The study has been approved by the University of Auckland Human Participants Ethics
> Committee (reference [UAHPEC #], [date]). I have attached the participant
> information sheet so you can see exactly what people would be agreeing to.
>
> I am also looking for a small number of people who work in dementia or aged care,
> and — with a family member or support person present throughout — a small number of
> people living with early-stage dementia. I would welcome a conversation about
> whether either of those would be appropriate through your service.
>
> Happy to answer any questions, or to meet if that would be easier.
>
> Ngā mihi,
> Richman Tan
> […contact…]
> Supervisor: Assoc. Prof. Jing Sun, […contact…]

## 2. Invitation for the organisation to forward

*Kept short. This is the message a carer actually reads.*

**Subject:** Help test an app for finding dementia information — University of Auckland

> Kia ora,
>
> Researchers at the University of Auckland are looking for family carers to try out an
> app that answers questions about dementia care, and to tell them what they think.
>
> The app answers in two ways: as text on the screen, and as an animated character that
> speaks the answer out loud. They want to know which is easier to use.
>
> **What is involved**
> - About 35–45 minutes, online, on your own device, whenever suits you
> - You will be given six short made-up situations and asked to use the app to find
>   information about them
> - Some short questions about how easy it was to use
> - You can take a break, skip anything, or stop at any time
>
> **You do not need to talk about your own situation.** The situations are made up. No
> names or contact details are collected, and there is no payment.
>
> You will need a device with a microphone, an internet connection, and Google Chrome
> or Microsoft Edge.
>
> If you are interested, email Richman Tan at […] and he will send you the information
> sheet and a link.
>
> Approved by the University of Auckland Human Participants Ethics Committee,
> reference [UAHPEC #], [date].

## 3. Invitation for health and aged-care workers

**Subject:** 40 minutes of your time — testing a dementia information tool

> Kia ora,
>
> I am a final-year engineering student at the University of Auckland. I have built an
> app that answers dementia-care questions from a library of trusted New Zealand
> information, and I am evaluating two ways of delivering those answers: as text, and
> through an animated character that speaks.
>
> I am looking for a small number of people who work in dementia or aged care to take
> part alongside a larger group of family carers. Your professional view on whether the
> answers are accurate, appropriately cautious, and usable is particularly valuable.
>
> **What is involved:** about 35–45 minutes, online, on your own device, at a time that
> suits you. Six fictional scenarios, then some short questions.
>
> **This is not an assessment of you or your practice.** Your employer is not involved
> and will not be told whether you took part. The scenarios are fictional — please do
> not enter details of anyone in your care.
>
> No names collected, no payment, stop at any time.
>
> If you are interested, email Richman Tan at […].
>
> Approved by the University of Auckland Human Participants Ethics Committee,
> reference [UAHPEC #], [date].
