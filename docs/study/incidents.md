# Incident Log

Referenced by `ethics/data-management-plan.md` §8 and
`ethics/risk-and-distress-protocol.md` §4. **Empty is the expected state.**

Record anything that would have to be disclosed to UAHPEC or reported in the
write-up, whether or not it changed the results:

- an unsafe, fabricated, or inappropriately confident answer from the system;
- a participant disclosing risk to themselves or others;
- exposure of study data;
- a technical failure that cost a participant their session;
- any deviation from the approved protocol.

Add newest first. Do not delete entries. An incident that turns out to be
harmless still stays in the log — suppressing it would misstate the safety record
of a healthcare-adjacent tool, and the record is the point.

**Suppression is the failure mode this file exists to prevent.** If you find
yourself deciding an incident is too minor to write down, write it down.
