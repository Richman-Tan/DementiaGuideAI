Avatar lip-sync rating

About this task

This short task is part of a final-year student project that built an AI guide for people caring for someone with dementia. The guide is a computer-generated character who speaks its answers aloud. You will watch about 11 pairs of short videos (5–10 seconds each) of the character saying the same sentence twice, and for each video you will rate how well the mouth movements match the speech and how natural they look, then pick the one that looks more natural. It takes about 10–15 minutes. There are no right or wrong answers, and you are rating the computer character, not yourself. Taking part is voluntary: you can stop at any time without giving a reason, and if you stop before the end nothing is kept unless you ask for it to be. Please do not type your name or anything that identifies you; use only the code the researcher gives you. Your answers are saved as an anonymous file on this computer and used only for the project's evaluation.

Please use headphones or speakers with the sound on, and sit at a normal distance from the screen.

Please use the short code you were given (letters and numbers only), not your name.
