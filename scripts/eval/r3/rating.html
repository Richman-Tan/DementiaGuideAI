About this task

DementiaGuide is a final-year engineering student project. It is an AI assistant that answers questions from people caring for someone with dementia in New Zealand. We are asking one health-care worker who had no part in building it to rate a set of its answers, so we can check that our own ratings hold up.

  • What you do: for each of 60 answers you read the question, the answer and the material the assistant was given. Then you score each aspect listed (for example tone or safety) 0, 1 or 2, using the description shown next to each score. That is 199 scores in total, with an optional comment on any answer.
  • How long: about 3 hours in total, roughly 3 minutes per answer. You do not need to do it in one go. Your progress is saved in this browser as you work, so you can close the tab and come back later with the same link, on the same device and browser.
  • Anonymous: we do not ask your name, and please do not type names anywhere. You enter only the short code from Richman's message. Apart from the answers on this screen, nothing about you is recorded.
  • What it is used for: your scores are compared with the project's other ratings of the same answers. The result goes into the project's final report, where you are described only as "a health-care worker independent of the project".
  • At the end you download one small file and email it to Richman, the person who sent you this link. Nothing is sent anywhere automatically.
  • Voluntary: you can stop at any time without giving a reason. If you stop, just do not send the file.

The questions were written for testing and are not about real patients. Some describe distressing situations, such as a medical emergency or a carer under strain. The answers were produced by the assistant during testing and are not checked or corrected; some may be wrong, and that is part of what you are rating.

This browser is not letting the page save your progress (for example in a private window). Your scores will be lost if you close or reload the tab. Please use a normal window, or finish in one sitting.
Please use the short code you were given (letters then numbers), not your name.
